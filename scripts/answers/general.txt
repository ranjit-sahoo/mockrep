Tell me about yourself.|Use present, past, future in about 60 to 90 seconds. Example: "I am [name], a [your role] with [x years or fresher] experience. Recently I worked on [your main skill or project] and I am good at [two strengths]. Earlier I [studied or worked at ...]. I want this role because [reason linked to the job], and I want to grow in [area]." Use your own real details and practise it aloud.
Why do you want this role and this company?|Give two specific reasons. Example: "The role uses [skill] which I enjoy and have practised in [project]. I also like that [company] works on [product or field] and gives a chance to learn [thing]. I read about [one real fact about the company], and I believe I can contribute from the first month." Do not mention only salary.
What is your biggest strength and a weakness you are fixing?|Example: "My strength is [reliable follow-through or quick learning], for example [one proof]. My weakness was [a real, small one such as speaking up in meetings]. I fixed it by [specific steps], and now [progress]." Choose a real weakness that does not block the job, and show the steps.
Tell me about a time you failed.|Use STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Example: "In [project] I [mistake, e.g. missed a deadline because I underestimated the work]. I told my manager early, fixed the most important part first, and delivered the rest a day later. Since then I add a buffer to my estimates and share progress daily." Own the mistake and show what you changed.
Describe a conflict with a colleague or manager.|Use STAR and stay professional. Example: "A teammate and I disagreed on [how to do a task]. I listened to their reason, explained mine with facts, and we agreed on a short trial. The result was [outcome], and our working relationship became better." Never blame the other person.
How do you handle tight deadlines and many priorities?|Say your method, then an example. Example: "I list everything, mark urgent and important tasks, and agree the order with my manager. I finish the high impact work first, tell people early if something will slip, and avoid distractions. In [project] I handled [three tasks] in [time] this way and delivered on time."
Where do you see yourself in five years?|Link growth to the role and stay realistic. Example: "In five years I want to be a strong [senior version of this role] who can guide others. I plan to keep learning [relevant skills] and take bigger responsibility step by step. I hope to do this with a company like yours."
