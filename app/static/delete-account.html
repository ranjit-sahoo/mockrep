Delete your MockRep account

This page is part of the MockRep account controls.

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. It removes:

  • your login (email, password hash and linked Google sign-in)
  • everything you saved to your history (answers, prep packs, reviews, resumes)
  • your progress scores over time
  • all login sessions on every device
  • your password-recovery code, your Pro waitlist spot and any company requests you sent

If you are a recruiter account owner, deleting your account also removes your company data: campaigns, screening results, pipeline entries and team setup.

Guest data (used without an account) is removed automatically within 2 days and is not affected. Recruiter screening answers you gave as a candidate follow the retention chosen by that recruiter (7-90 days) or are removed when you delete your account, whichever comes first.
